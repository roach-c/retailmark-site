We work in the systems your buyer already trusts

A number is only worth as much as the place it came from. Everything we build sits in the tools the category already uses, so it can be checked rather than taken on faith.

Retail Link

Walmart's supplier portal, and the single source everyone in the room is looking at. Sales, in stock, store level performance and the reports a buyer pulls before a meeting. We work in it daily, so a claim we make is a claim you can check in the same screen.

Item 360

Where an item is actually born: UPCs, case configuration, dimensions, weights, images and copy. Most first time suppliers lose weeks here, because a rejected submission does not come back with an explanation. It just sits.

Nielsen

Category and market measurement beyond one retailer. It answers the question a buyer will ask about whether your growth is real or borrowed from somewhere else in the category.

IRi

Syndicated point of sale data used to read velocity, promotion lift and competitive share. Useful for building the case that your item earns its slot rather than merely fills it.

Canopy

Supplier reporting and analytics used to keep performance visible between reviews, so nothing is discovered at the point it becomes a problem.

Atlas

Assortment and space planning. Where the modular actually gets built, and where an item either has a home or does not.
